## 🚀 About Me

Technical lead and entrepreneur based in **Nairobi, Kenya**. I'm **Lead Data Engineer & Product Owner at [ArdhiSasa](https://ardhisasa.lands.go.ke/)**, Kenya's National Land Information Management System, where I lead a team of eight, own the product backlog (EDMS, database infrastructure, data warehouse and pipelines) and run delivery in two-week sprints with a release per sprint.

I'm also founder of **[Sheria Platform](https://github.com/Sheria-Platform/sheria_platform_mvp)**. 9+ years spanning IoT platforms, cloud infrastructure, data engineering and self-hosted AI systems — and still hands-on in the architecture.

> *"Data engineering: where 90% of your time is waiting for pipelines to finish, and 10% is fixing why they failed."*

## 🎯 Highlights

- 🏛️ **[ArdhiSasa](https://ardhisasa.lands.go.ke/)** — lead the team and own the product for Kenya's national land registry data platform; led the platform through a national Auditor-General audit with a positive assessment
- 📄 **EDMS** — our in-house Electronic Document Management System, built for the ministry's unique data challenges, with automated ETL and a PostgreSQL backend; 5M+ land records digitized
- 🧭 **One parcel, one reference number** — advocated a single reference number across all departments and built the router engine to convert between them; now the ministry standard
- ⚖️ **[Sheria Platform](https://github.com/Sheria-Platform/sheria_platform_mvp)** — agentic RAG judicial intelligence system for Kenya's courts, self-hosted end to end (Oct 2025 – present); shortlisted in the NIRU National AI Hackathon 2025 ([architecture tour](https://danielmalungu.dev/blog/posts/sheria-platform-architecture-tour))
- 🏥 **[Mwangaza HIMS](https://hims.danielmalungu.dev/)** — hospital information system for Kenyan clinics, with a live demo
- 💧 **IoT Smart Water Metering** — NB-IoT smart meter system with real-time incident detection, cutting water losses by 30% for two utility companies

More project details and full work history on my [website](https://danielmalungu.dev/).
